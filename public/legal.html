Privacy notice

The Full Body publisher identified in the app-store listing is the data controller. Contact the publisher through the support details in that listing.

What we collect

We collect an email address and account identifier; preferred name, age, avatar gender selection, units, height, current weight and goal weight; and routines, exercises, sets, weights, repetitions, duration, notes, dates, completion, and calories you enter. Supabase handles credentials and secure account records. The web build uses local storage and the native app uses secure storage and a private SQLite database.

Why and where

We use the data to provide account access, offline workout logging, routine planning, progress comparisons, and sync. Account and training data is synced over encrypted connections to Supabase in its eu-west-1 Ireland region. Body measurements and workout records can reveal health information; the mobile onboarding asks for separate explicit consent. Security processing protects the service.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal data or use advertising identifiers, targeted advertising, behavioural analytics, tracking pixels, precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, HealthKit, Health Connect, biometric identification, or connected wearable data.

Retention and rights

Data is kept while the account is active. Profile provides export and account deletion. Infrastructure backups and limited legal or security records may remain only as reasonably required. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object, obtain a portable copy, withdraw consent, and complain to a regulator. We do not discriminate for exercising a right.

Other protections

Full Body is for adults aged 18 or older. It does not make medical or similarly significant automated decisions. Row-level access rules, secure token storage, encrypted transport, and least-privilege access protect data. Reportable breaches will be disclosed as law requires. Material changes will be shown before new processing begins.

You must be 18 or older. Full Body is a self-directed workout-planning and logging tool. Its exercise information, comparisons, avatar projection, and calorie figures are informational and are not medical advice, treatment, physiotherapy, or a guarantee of results.

Choose activity appropriate for you and stop for pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms. Seek qualified advice for injuries, pregnancy, health conditions, medication concerns, or uncertainty. Do not use the app for emergencies.

You own the data you enter and permit us to process it only to provide and secure the service. Do not misuse the service or violate others' rights. The app, original interface, text, brand, and artwork are protected; third-party components keep their own licences. Availability is not guaranteed. Nothing here limits mandatory consumer rights or liability that law does not allow us to exclude. You may delete your account at any time.

Cookie and device-storage policy

The mobile app uses secure storage for sign-in tokens and SQLite for profile and workout data. The web build uses local storage for requested workout history and authentication. These are necessary for sign-in, offline saving, and sync.

The current app has no advertising, cross-site tracking, personalisation, or analytics cookies. Because only necessary storage is used, no opt-in banner is shown. Any future non-essential storage will require clear information and prior opt-in where law requires it. You can sign out, delete the account, clear app storage, or clear browser site data.

Refund policy

The current app is free and has no charge, subscription, trial, in-app purchase, renewal, or hidden fee, so there is no purchase to refund. Before any future paid feature, the price, billing period, renewal and cancellation terms, and trial conditions will be shown before purchase.

Future store purchases may be handled under Apple or Google refund rules. This policy never reduces statutory refund, cancellation, repair, replacement, or other consumer rights.
