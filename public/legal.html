Privacy notice

How Full Body uses and protects account, body-measurement, and workout data.

Web: no account is required. Workout history, notes, duration, body weight used for a calorie estimate, and calculated progress stay in this browser's local storage. The web app does not send this data to Full Body or Supabase. Mobile: the app uses an account and can sync the data described below.

Who is responsible

Ardi Bandilli is the independent individual developer operating the Full Body project and is responsible for the personal data described here. For privacy or support enquiries, email ardibandilli2@gmail.com. This notice is version 2026-09-29.

Data we collect

The mobile app collects only information needed to create an account, build a routine, record workouts, and sync progress:

  • Account data: email address, account identifier, and authentication records. Supabase handles the password; Full Body does not store a readable copy.
  • Profile data: preferred name, age, gender selection, unit preference, height, current weight, and goal weight.
  • Training data: routine choices, exercises, sets, weights, repetitions, duration, notes, workout dates, completion status, and calories entered or calculated from entries.
  • Necessary technical data: encrypted sign-in tokens, local sync state, and security or error records produced when the mobile service is used.

The web app does not request an email, account identifier, preferred name, age, height, gender, or goal weight.

Data we do not collect

The current products do not use advertising identifiers, analytics pixels, third-party behavioural analytics, precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, biometric identifiers, HealthKit, Health Connect, or connected wearable data. We do not sell personal data or use it for targeted advertising.

Why we use data

  • On mobile, to create and secure the account, keep the user signed in, and sync data across devices.
  • To create the requested routine and show workout history and comparisons.
  • To save preferences and provide offline use.
  • On mobile, to maintain security, diagnose failures, comply with law, and respond to rights requests.

Where UK or EU data-protection law applies, mobile account and service data is processed to perform our agreement with you, and security processing is based on our legitimate interest in protecting the service. Body measurements and workout records may reveal health information, so the mobile app asks for separate explicit consent before collecting them. That consent may be withdrawn by deleting the mobile account and data.

Storage and sharing

The web app stores workout data only in this browser's local storage and offers JSON export and import. It has no authentication or cloud sync. Anyone with access to the same browser profile may be able to view that data.

The mobile app keeps an offline copy in its private SQLite database. Sign-in tokens are kept in secure device storage. Mobile account and training data are sent over encrypted connections to Supabase, our authentication and database processor; the current project is hosted in the eu-west-1 region in Ireland. We share data only with processors needed to operate the mobile service, when directed by the user, or when law requires it. Apple and Google separately process store, download, and device information under their own notices.

Retention and deletion

Web data remains until the user selects Delete all progress, clears browser site data, or the browser removes it. Full Body cannot restore or remotely delete web data because it never receives it. Export a backup before clearing data or changing devices.

Mobile account and training data is kept while the account is active. Use Delete account and data in Profile to remove the account and associated active cloud and local records. Infrastructure backups and limited legal or security records may remain only for the period reasonably required to restore systems, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, or meet legal duties.

Your choices and rights

The web app provides local export, import, and deletion controls. Mobile Profile provides data export and account deletion. Depending on where you live, you may also ask to access, correct, restrict, object to, or receive a portable copy of personal data, withdraw consent, and complain to your data-protection authority. We do not discriminate against anyone for exercising a privacy right.

Children

Full Body is for adults aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. Contact the publisher if you believe a child has created a mobile account.

Security, breaches, and decisions

The mobile service uses access controls, row-level database rules, encrypted transport, secure token storage, and least-privilege permissions. The web app limits its data path to local browser storage and does not provide a network account service. No system or device is perfectly secure. If a reportable breach involving data we control occurs, we will notify affected people and regulators as required.

The products calculate workout summaries from values the user enters. They do not make legal, medical, employment, credit, or similarly significant automated decisions.

Changes

We will update this notice before materially changing what we collect or why. If a change requires new consent, we will ask before the new processing begins.
