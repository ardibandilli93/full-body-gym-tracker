Web progress

The web app has no account or cloud record. Use Delete all progress in the web app's Your data panel, or clear site data in your browser settings. This permanently removes the workout history stored in that browser. Full Body cannot restore it, so export a backup first if you want a copy.

Mobile account

The mobile app lets you permanently delete your account without contacting support.

  1. Open the Full Body mobile app and sign in.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Select Delete account and data and confirm.

This deletes the authentication account and associated active cloud profile, routine, and workout records, plus the local copy on that device. Export from Profile first if you want a copy. Limited backup, fraud-prevention, or legal records may remain only where required.

If you cannot access the mobile account, email Ardi Bandilli at ardibandilli2@gmail.com. Include only the account email and do not send a password or workout details.
