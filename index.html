Why 7 exercises? Each day keeps a lower-body pattern, an upper-body push, an upper-body pull and supporting work for posterior chain, shoulders/arms/core. The routine uses 21 fixed movements so week-to-week overload comparisons stay clean. Optional swap: Bulgarian Split Squat can replace Hack Squat on Day C if you prefer unilateral work or do not have a hack-squat machine. ACSM 2026 guidance.
